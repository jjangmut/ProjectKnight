## 1. Independent Actual Video & Telemetry Inspection

The actual uploaded **`Stage1_DualCamera_BeltScroll_1x.mp4`** was inspected, **49.616667s, 2977 frames at 60fps, 1280x720**. Sampled video sequence at 1.5s cadence from 18–48s demonstrates that, starting ~27s to ~46s, protagonist and boss spend most of the duel fighting in a compact area, overlapping in projected silhouette. The camera does keep them on screen, but being inside viewport **does not prove that the player has room or agency to escape**. The smaller hero can be obscured by the boss.

**Crucial repo telemetry reanalysis:** `STUDIO_V3/evidence/V3-024-R1/CAMERA_VISIBILITY_TRACE.json` has **131 recorded samples** (reported boss total = 1319 physics frames), from which representative:
- 27.07s: Player `(x=78.34,z=-2.17)`, Boss `(x=77.98,z=0)`.
- 30.07s: Player `(x=77.45,z=-2.11)`, Boss `(x=77.53,z=-0.63)`.
- 36.07s: Player `(x=78.27,z=-2.07)`, Boss `(x=77.90,z=-0.67)`.
- 39.07s: Player `(x=79.12,z=-2.06)`, Boss `(x=78.06,z=-0.76)`.
- 45.07s: Player `(x=79.13,z=-2.17)`, Boss `(x=78.24,z=-1.02)`.

Analysis proved that the core defect is **Z-depth back-edge trapping (player around Z=-2.1) and physical corridor occlusion**:
1. `side_qv_boss.gd` only checked X-axis bounds (`x <= arena_min_x + 1.8 or x >= arena_max_x - 1.8`) and completely ignored the Z depth boundaries ($Z \in [-2.6, 2.6]$).
2. When the player stood at $Z = -2.1$, the boss stood at $Z = -1.8$. With the boss's collider radius ($0.65\text{m}$) and the arena top wall ($Z = -2.6$), the boss physically sealed the corridor, preventing player escape toward arena center.
3. Player dash did not disable enemy collision, meaning a cornered player attempting to dash past the boss collided with the boss's capsule collider and remained trapped.
4. Low sorting offset scaling ($0.25$) and small separation distance caused player and boss sprites to overlap and clip heavily when fighting on the same depth rail.

## 2. Implemented Code-Level Remedies

### Priority 1: Full 2D (X and Z) Arena Boundary & Corner Detection
- Full arena margins in `side_qv_boss.gd`:
  - $X$ boundaries: $X \in [69.2, 85.8]$
  - $Z$ boundaries: $Z \in [-2.6, 2.6]$
- Full 2D boundary proximity:
  - `player_near_x_wall`: within 3.2m of Left/Right gates
  - `player_near_z_wall`: within 1.6m of Top/Bottom depth rails ($Z \le -1.0$ or $Z \ge 1.0$)
  - `player_in_corner`: both $X$ and $Z$ wall proximity (all 4 corners)
  - `player_is_cornered`: any $X$ or $Z$ wall proximity

### Priority 2: Boss Escape Path Yielding & Center Retreat
- When `player_near_z_wall`:
  - If player is near Top wall ($Z \le -1.0$), Boss steps down toward $Z \ge 0.2$, opening a clear $\ge 1.6\text{m}$ corridor toward center.
  - If player is near Bottom wall ($Z \ge 1.0$), Boss steps up toward $Z \le -0.2$, opening a clear $\ge 1.6\text{m}$ corridor toward center.
  - If boss and player share the same vertical lane ($|X_{player} - X_{boss}| < 1.4\text{m}$), the boss yields horizontally in $X$.
- When `player_in_corner` (all 4 corners):
  - Standoff expands to $3.2\text{m}$.
  - Boss retreats toward arena center ($X \approx 77.5, Z \approx 0.0$), leaving all diagonal exit angles completely open.
- Anti-trap unstick repulsion:
  - In `_prevent_player_pushing()`, if player is near any wall, boss receives a strong $4.2\text{ m/s}$ repulsion vector straight toward arena center.

### Priority 3: Player Movement Agency During Combat Actions
- **Dash Phase-Through**:
  - Dashing disables Enemy Layer 2 collision (`set_collision_mask_value(2, false)`), allowing the player to dash directly through the boss to escape corners.
  - Layer 2 re-enabled upon dash completion with soft-push overlap separation (`_resolve_enemy_overlap()`).
- **Guard Walk**:
  - Tactical defensive movement enabled at 40% speed ($2.2\text{ m/s}$ in X, $1.4\text{ m/s}$ in Z) while keeping shield raised.
  - Immediate Dash cancel supported out of Guard state.
- **Attack Recovery Drift**:
  - Directional movement drift at 55% speed during late recovery ($t \le 0.10$s); zero input freezing after attack swings.

### Priority 4: Sprite Overlap Separation & Depth Polish
- Scaled `sprite_3d.sorting_offset` to `Z * 1.5` for clear, non-flickering depth layering.
- Added player foreground bias ($+0.05$) when Z coordinates are close, ensuring the player is never obscured behind the boss.
- Maintained classic 2.5D belt-scroll side-facing sprites (`flip_h`) during all $Z$ traversal.
