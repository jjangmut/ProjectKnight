## 2. Code-level hypotheses to validate before fixing

- `side_qv_boss.gd` lines ~268–288, ~367–370: `player_is_cornered` only checks **X bounds**, so Z-edge (-Z/+Z) never triggers boss standoff/Z lane yield. `boss_near_wall` similarly X-only. Patrol chase can maintain occupied Z lane indefinitely.
- `side_qv_boss.gd` `_prevent_player_pushing()`: seeks distance 1.55m and changes boss velocity, not necessarily free 2D lane. `_clamp_arena_bounds()` clamps boss after `move_and_slide`; post-solve position correction may repeatedly press on body/walls if contact not resolved.
- `side_qv_player.gd` `_prevent_enemy_pushing()` adds repulsive velocity inside min_dist and can oppose desired escape; state-specific input `State.ATTACK` applies `move_toward(..., 0, 30*delta)` suppressing locomotion, `State.GUARD/HURT` and dash recovery may also restrict; input bot spams parry/attack. Need diagnose how many frames player's **input direction is nonzero but actual movement along that direction is tiny**, not just current HP and boss location.
- `side_qv_stage_1.gd` arena rear/front gate x=68/87, boss X clamp [69.2,85.8], Z [−2.6,+2.6]. Global Z walkway colliders (rear parapet, front fence) plus player effective limit may leave short evasive corridor. Measure physical free-width relative to combined character radii and dodge distance.
- Boss `CHARGE_ACTIVE` retains X-only charging, `TRIPLE_CLEAVE` / `QUAKE_RING` may occupy Z escape zones. Ensure dodge route is visually legible and not physically blocked by boss hitbox, ring, decorative pillars or edge.
- If issue is *apparent* cornering because boss sprite occludes hero although the player can move, fix art scale/foreground ordering / ground shadow visibility in addition to physical collision, not replace one with the other.

## 4. Targeted game-code remedies, conditional on reproduction

- Make boss standoff/attack selection **2D arena-edge aware**. Detect edge using true physical bounds of hero and boss + radii on BOTH X and Z. Make boss yield an actual side step / retreat or complete a recovery animation opening a path; do not fake it with unconditional position teleports or direct HP/state manipulation.
- During shield charge near wall or when player is pressed into any X/Z boundary, prevent ongoing contact pressure. Avoid edge trap loops; allow a readable parry/dodge/punish opportunity. Collision response should never force hero through wall; collision remains on.
- Ensure player has **escape agency** while attacking/guarding: if controls lock during attack, make deliberate short cancel/stamina rules clear and introduce a valid dash cancel or post-hit escape; never auto-attack-lock or hold guard forever. Confirm physical movement in X/Z when intended and not stunned. Preserve attack direction, hitbox, counter, projectile, pillar and pogo mechanics.
- Improve visual silhouette when hero stands behind boss: adjust body spacing, actual boss Sprite3D projected scale/depth sorting and outlines/shadows as necessary at 1280x720. Do not make actors semi-transparent by default or crop scene just to game metrics.
- Test actual usable area, not center pixel on-screen percentage. No more large camera policy rewrite unless camera is contributing to actual lost player control.
