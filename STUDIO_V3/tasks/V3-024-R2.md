## 0. Problem Context & Defect Trace

Direct player gameplay analysis and audit of `CAMERA_VISIBILITY_TRACE.json` revealed:
- **Depth Rail Trapping**: In V3-024-R1, the player spent **90.1% of the entire boss fight** (118 of 131 samples) pinned along the upper depth rail ($Z \le -1.5$, average $Z = -1.95$, min $Z = -2.45$).
- **One-Dimensional Corner Detection**: `side_qv_boss.gd` only checked $X$-axis bounds (`x <= arena_min_x + 1.8 or x >= arena_max_x - 1.8`) while completely ignoring the $Z$ depth boundaries ($Z \in [-2.6, 2.6]$).
- **Narrow Depth Corridor Blockade**: When the player was at $Z = -2.1$, the boss stepped into $Z = -1.8$. With the boss's collider radius ($0.65\text{m}$) and the arena top wall ($Z = -2.6$), the boss physically sealed the entire corridor, blocking the player from escaping back to the center of the arena.
- **Dash Collision Wall**: Player dash did not disable enemy collision, meaning a cornered player attempting to dash past the boss collided with the boss's capsule collider and remained trapped.
- **Continuous Sprite Overlap**: Low sorting offset scaling ($0.25$) and small separation distance caused player and boss sprites to overlap and clip heavily when fighting on the same depth rail.

## 1. Core Implementation Requirements

### Priority 1: Full 2D (X and Z) Arena Boundary & Corner Detection
- Define full arena margins in `side_qv_boss.gd`:
  - $X$ boundaries: $X \in [69.2, 85.8]$
  - $Z$ boundaries: $Z \in [-2.6, 2.6]$
- Detect:
  - `player_near_x_wall`: within 2.0m of Left/Right gates
  - `player_near_z_wall`: within 1.0m of Top/Bottom depth rails ($Z \le -1.6$ or $Z \ge 1.6$)
  - `player_in_corner`: both $X$ and $Z$ wall proximity (all 4 corners)
  - `player_is_cornered`: any $X$ or $Z$ wall proximity

### Priority 2: Boss Escape Path Yielding & Standoff
- When `player_near_z_wall`:
  - If player is near Top wall ($Z \le -1.6$), Boss clamps boss $Z \ge -0.6$, yielding a clear $\ge 1.5\text{m}$ escape corridor toward center.
  - If player is near Bottom wall ($Z \ge 1.6$), Boss clamps boss $Z \le 0.6$, yielding a clear $\ge 1.5\text{m}$ escape corridor toward center.
- When `player_in_corner` (all 4 corners):
  - Boss increases standoff distance to $\ge 3.0\text{m}$.
  - Boss moves toward arena center ($X \approx 77.5, Z \approx 0.0$), never trapping the corner exit angle.
- Anti-trap unstick leash:
  - If player remains near any wall for $> 0.4$s while boss is close, boss actively steps back toward center at 3.5 m/s.

### Priority 3: Player Movement Agency During Combat Actions
- **Dash Phase-Through**:
  - Dashing disables Enemy Layer 2 collision (`set_collision_mask_value(2, false)`), allowing the player to dash directly through the boss to escape corners.
  - Re-enabled upon dash completion with soft-push overlap separation.
- **Guard Walk**:
  - Allow slow tactical repositioning ($40\%$ speed, $2.2\text{ m/s}$ in X, $1.4\text{ m/s}$ in Z) while holding Guard.
  - Immediate Dash cancel allowed out of Guard state.
- **Attack Recovery Drift**:
  - Allow directional movement input during attack recovery ($t \le 0.10$s) and subtle directional drift during swings.

### Priority 4: Sprite Overlap Separation & Depth Polish
- Increase `min_dist` in `_prevent_player_pushing()` from $1.55\text{m}$ to $1.95\text{m}$.
- Scale `sprite_3d.sorting_offset` to `Z * 1.5` for clear, non-flickering depth layering.
- Add player foreground bias ($+0.05$) when Z coordinates are tied, ensuring the player is never obscured behind the boss.
