## A. Critical finding from *actual current source* — prioritize this

This is not a problem that more particles, damage numbers or more frequent camera shake will solve.

**Verified current V3-025 code**:
1. `CLIENT/Game/scripts/side_quarter_view/side_qv_player.gd`:
   - `ATTACK_TOTAL_DURATION = 0.24`s for all 3 combo stages (line ~53).
   - `start_attack()` (~784–854) immediately enables `attack_area_3d.monitoring = true`, calls `_check_melee_hit()` at ~848 **on attack start frame**, and spawns combo-3 beam immediately. This lets damage occur before the depicted sword travels to target; it is the **first core combat-feel bug**. Audit any deferred physics overlap handling separately; direct spatial check is immediate.
   - A single 2x2 **four-frame** `TexAttack = attack_v1_aligned.png` is reused for every combo stage with different **VFX tweens** only. Each swing therefore looks essentially identical except arc shape. `_update_character_animation()` (~1542–1548) assigns attack frames by linear fraction of 0.24s. 4 frames in 0.24s cannot communicate clear distinct heavy windup/contact/settle poses with reliable hit markers when damage happens at t=0.
   - `_check_melee_hit()` uses Area3D overlap plus a broad direct reach test and attacks every candidate once per call; hit VFX / popups / audio / `GameFeelManager.hit_stop_3d()` come after immediate damage. Hitstop cannot fix **incorrect contact timing**.
   - Attack recovery movement and cancel logic exists and must survive.
2. `side_qv_enemy.gd`: attack phase timer 0.28 TELL, 0.14 ACTIVE, 0.22 RECOVERY, yet animation uses a 3x2 sprite sheet with one-frame tell (frame 3), strike (4), recovery (5). Key character action is mostly static while floor warning moves.
3. `side_qv_boss.gd`: 4x3 12-frame atlas; during TRIPLE_CLEAVE, frames 8–11 loop at 12fps, largely decoupled from the actual 3-hit phase timeline; animations cannot distinguish each unique anticipation/contact/recovery; CHARGE uses defensive idle sequence. Need attack-state → authored pose timeline, not generic loop.
4. `side_qv_camera.gd` includes `Engine.time_scale = 0.05` global hitstop method; `GameFeelManager.hit_stop_3d` separately invoked by player. Audit all global time scale changes, coroutines and competing hitstops. Do not stack global pauses and corrupt input timing, camera tracking, projectile response, screen recording provenance.
5. Earlier user-directed art policy: **side-facing sprites facing left/right at all Z locations**; no front/back art switch. Boss / hero same projected footline during actual melee, Z dodge and corner escape preserved.
6. V3-025 `PACING_POLICY_COMPARISON.json` has internal inconsistencies (e.g. `cleared=true` alongside `final_player_hp=0`, `enemies_defeated=0`, zero confirmed parries despite report claiming 8). Rebuild same-run instrumentation; do not claim a credible feel score until this is corrected.

**Desired outcome:** readable authored poses → frame-exact collision → causal hit response → satisfying sound/animation/VFX → fair cancels and tactical depth. A *combat event timeline system* + *reproducible animation content pipeline* is required.

## C. Phase 1: Mandatory combat architecture before art expansion

Refactor/introduce shared **CombatActionDefinition** resource and **CombatTimelineController** that centralize attack timing and events, without breaking the already working State/AI/collision architecture:
- Data: action_id, attacker_kind, direction (left/right art; X/Z physical hit direction independently), total duration, fps/frames, indexed **pose/frame keys**, startup/active/recovery frame ranges, attack lock, root-motion curve, hitbox shape/offset/size, push/knockback, VFX/sound/contact marker, hitstop profile, cancel windows (on hit / whiff / blocked), combo buffer, optional guard-break/stagger.
- One authoritative timeline clock updated in physics. Frame stepping need not be 1 fixed engine tick per art pose, but state transitions & active hitbox are tied to marker times/ticks; `AnimationPlayer` method tracks or custom marker resource must be backed by deterministic physics events.
- `attack_begin` → authored WINDUP (hitbox OFF) → `hitbox_open` when visible blade enters effective range → one resolved `contact` event per attack/target (blocked, parried, hit or whiff) → `hitbox_close` → authored follow-through → recovery/cancel → idle/next step. Attack timer **must never deal damage on startup frame** unless intentionally instant action explicitly modeled.
- Refactor `side_qv_player.gd` first, then grunt, shield knight, crossbow, boss without duplicate hand-coded attack timing. Keep all previously approved mechanics functional and explicitly make rollback possible. Do not merge giant untested rewrites.
- Physics `Area3D.get_overlapping_bodies()` can be a frame behind; provide synchronized `PhysicsDirectSpaceState3D` shape/raycast/overlap or deferred area list measured during active frames, with deterministic test. Never grant a hit from a target behind attacker or outside Z lane. Prevent duplicated hit events. Contact point should be computed on target capsule/weapon reach, not a fixed spark 0.95m above all targets.
- Keep display `Sprite3D` and world collision separate; frame events can be driven by a *per-action* visual rig (see D). No arbitrary player/boss teleport, perpetual hitstun, body clipping, or camera art tricks.
- `GameFeelManager.hit_stop_3d` and global time-scale calls must be audited: implement a scoped centralized **HitPauseCoordinator** (per-attacker and victim render/animation holds, not whole-world global time_scale for normal melee; optional boss special constrained pause). Aggregate overlapping contacts deterministically; preserve true 1x capture timeline and responsive buffered inputs. If global pause remains for rare special, document interaction with timers/process and no unbounded overlap.

## E. Phase 3: Action design, hit reactions and sensory layering

Introduce **one shared HitResult event** with values: NONE/WHIFF, HIT_LIGHT, HIT_HEAVY, BLOCK, JUST_PARRY, ARMOR, GUARD_BREAK, KILL. All systems react to that same confirmed contact timestamp exactly once: victim sprite pose, attacker recoil/follow-through, physical knockback, damage, VFX, hitpause, camera, sound. No unsynced independent timers.

**Initial adjustable design starting points**, NOT mandatory final numbers (iterate by footage and human feedback):
- Hero first quick attack: ~0.08s anticipatory pose, ~0.07–0.10s active sweep, ~0.11–0.16s recovery (total ~0.30–0.34s).
- Hero Attack2 reverse slash: distinct prep direction, ~0.09–0.12s startup, ~0.08–0.10s active, ~0.12–0.16s recover.
- Hero Attack3 finisher: ~0.14–0.18s weight shift/raised blade, ~0.09–0.12s active, ~0.20–0.30s committed recovery, conditional hit/guard cancel.
- Minor enemy hitstop local 30–45ms; heavy/guard-break 60–90ms; JUST PARRY 65–100ms. Boss contact slightly stronger only if no spam. Visual frame hold should be readable but not freeze game input.
- Contact reaction within at most 1 render frame of true hit; distinguish light recoil vs heavy stumble vs armor absorb vs shield block. Impact sparkle short and at actual contact area, no giant screen-covering burst; enemy has meaningful directional velocity response with physical caps and edge safety.
- Camera impulse modest, primarily along hit direction, diminishing returns with many hits. No repeated large randomized rotation during 3-hit combo or unbounded trauma. A/B test no-shake vs tuned and ensure both still readable.
- Audio **must** be differentiated: dry weapon SWISH on active, tangible body/armor THUD on verified contact, metallic CLANG on block, sharp resonant parry, heavier bass on finisher, optional subtle cloth/footplant. Choose/create permitted original/licensed SFX; document source/license, loudness/peak, dynamic mixing/voice stealing, mono/stereo positioning. Never play hit sound on a whiff.
- Distinct player input feel: respond to button within next simulation frame by entering windup, with **contact delayed** to blade marker. Buffer chains and cancel windows intentional; no huge input lock, no infinite spam; guard/cancel during designated recovery only. Whiff recovery may be longer than hit-confirm follow-through but no unnecessary delay.
- Enemy/body death: stagger/head/shoulders and falling pose; don't instantly vanish on HP zero. Preserve victory and death/retry signal timing while animation plays. Crossbow bolts remain physically correct.
- Add optional settings: reduce shake/flash and hitpause for accessibility; maintain clear geometry and gameplay timing even if these cosmetics are turned off.
