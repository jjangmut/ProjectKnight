## 3. Gate C — Enemy AI and combat pacing, without trivial HP inflation

- Keep Stage1 >=3 distinct encounter zones and 3 enemy archetypes/12-ish enemies (adapt counts only if measured quality improves). Give each meaningful tactical behavior and readable startup/active/recovery. Enemy should be dangerous *for a moving player who attacks without guarding*, but avoid impossible damage, spawn unfairness and unavoidable stun chains. Shield enemy should reward flanking, ranged opponent should force lateral or timed counters, melee group should use spacing/turn-taking so scene is intelligible.
- Add quality differentiation per zone: foreground/background props, terrain silhouettes, obstacles and changes to movement/attack decisions. Not just same tiled 100m hallway with three `PROCEED >>` gates. Ensure colliders on solid geometry; protect 2.5D alternate lanes.
- Compare input policies on same build at multiple fixed seeds: 1) forward+attack spam (not stationary), 2) X/Z+attack no defense, 3) full move+dash+guard/parry+attack, 4) idle/other diagnostics. Log deaths, stage progress, damage, real successful parries, *actually executed* dashes, human-visible cue response, boss phase progression. Do not make impossible baselines pass by staying at spawn.
- **Pacing target**: first-time human Stage1 about 2–4 minutes as initial design hypothesis. Expert automated clears can be shorter; no arbitrary time padding, artificial wait or HP inflation. Measure encounter and traversal pacing on real rendered play. Current 30.82s automated clear is a clear signal to reassess how trivial AI/controller may be. Track fraction of active combat and idle travel and tune via decisions/counterplay.
- Test clean start, death/retry, repeated encounters, recovery pickups vs combat attrition. Avoid unbounded orb drops that erase challenge.

## 4. Gate D — Boss encounter that functions as a fight

- Preserve boss 3 distinct attacks but **visible, timed** start/active/recovery and reachable counter strategy. Introduce fight beats/phase transition only if needed for actual player readability/engagement; patterns should arise from the boss AI, not direct invocations. Mark each warning and hit event frame, show which player input dodged/parried vs suffered damage.
- Boss should not die from an uninterrupted same-spot combo in ~6.5sec. Fix through readable defensive windows, spacing, stun resistance and attack recovery (not simply enormous HP); do not make it a health sponge.
- Visualize and resolve sprite overlay/collision during close combat; preserve correct floor Y: no unintended pogo bounce; jump still functions.
- Compare boss-only attack-spam vs movement+defense *after legitimate reach to boss*; policy and event logs must expose exactly what happened.
