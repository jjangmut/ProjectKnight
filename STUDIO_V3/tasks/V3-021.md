### Genre decision

Build a **2.5D belt-scrolling action game** (e.g. streets-of-rage-like navigation), not a fixed-X 2D platformer and not unrestricted FPS/free camera: **X = horizontal forward progress; Z = traversable floor depth (up/down on screen); Y = vertical jump height/gravity**. Preserve readable side/3-quarter perspective, player-centered follow camera and safe arena rails. Input Left/Right -> X; Up/Down -> Z; Jump -> Y. Do not use up/down for menus or drop-through while walking unless explicit separate gesture/airborne mode. Existing 3D floor should become a meaningful playable lane, not cosmetic scenery.

## 1. Strategy — prototype Stage 1 FIRST, then assess expansion (do not shallow-fix 5 stages)

**Deliverable for V3-021 is a polished, playable Stage 1 redesign** plus reusable core systems and an honest regression impact report for Stage2-5. ### A. Foundation: genuine Z-depth 2.5D movement (P0)

- Implement player ground movement in X/Z with normalized diagonal input, acceleration/deceleration, meaningful speed; preserve Y jump, gravity, dodge, guard. Use `move_and_slide()` and collisions against floor and depth rails. Player footprint must stay at proper ground plane, and shadow tracks X/Z ground position while jump only changes Y.
- Update mobile joystick mapping to true 2-axis X/Z without stealing attack/guard buttons; preserve pointer ownership/focus loss. Keyboard: A/D or arrows L/R for X, W/S or arrows U/D for Z, Space jump, current combat keys. Provide one unobtrusive updated first-play hint.
- Change camera to follow X and readable Z depth with smooth panning, bounds, focus and no jitter. Ensure Stage1 genuinely scrolls beyond one 1280px screen; camera cannot reveal empty black world. Visibility of near/far enemies, ground contact and overlap must be correct.
- Break out 2.5D movement/facing/hit validation helpers so migrated enemies/projectiles can reuse later. Remove **unconditional** `Z=0` assignments only from Stage1 participant code paths. Audit stage-level `_ensure_arena_boundary_colliders` so it does not block forward progression before actual stage end.

### B. Combat hit direction and VFX accuracy (P0)

- Make an explicit 3D horizontal attack direction `Vector3(dir_x,0,dir_z)` from last meaningful X/Z movement or aim, plus a clear animation facing convention. Right/Left sprite flip is **not sufficient** for front/back attacks: choose legible front/back/side presentation using existing sprite directions or explicit alternative mirrored framing. If directional sprite assets are unavailable, do **not** claim proper 4-way directional animation; implement honest readable directional placeholder while creating asset-gap list.
- Attack hitboxes are oriented/placed in X/Z consistently with visual slash origin and hit travel; use oriented `Area3D`/physics query and lane/depth tolerance. Visual arc transform, sprite, contact sparks, knockback, guard wedge, dash vector and enemy reaction must agree. Remove damage to targets demonstrably outside the swing cone, above/below in depth or behind a single-direction attack.
- Capture matched frames from attacks facing X+, X-, Z+, Z-, diagonals. Verify actual hit/no-hit against test enemies in each orientation and compare VFX center with hitbox/debug geometries. Avoid global Z=0 assumptions in `side_qv_enemy.gd`, Stage1 boss and player attacks as necessary.
- Keep jump vs depth separation: Y airborne should not move attack/hitboxes into unrelated lanes, intentional aerial attack and stomp rules documented.

### C. Actual stage travel: 3–5 distinct camera-width segments and progression gates (P0)

- Replace Stage 1's single 38-unit arena with **at least 3 camera-widths** of traversable scrolling corridor (prefer 4–6 screens if time permits), visually distinct **entry approach -> patrol encounter -> varied encounter with hazard -> pre-boss approach -> boss arena**. Use modular 3D scenery/geometry already available; add cover/terrain variation without sacrificing legibility.
- Use progression/camera/encounter triggers based on player X and locale, not global stage-wide spawn all at once. Gate forward travel only during a clearly announced encounter; open after encounter ends; player can meaningfully move back within bounds. Add obvious forward navigation and wave clear cues. No invisible wall mid-walk.
- Encounter plan **minimum 8–12 distinct normal enemies total before boss, deployed in at least 3 encounters** with 2–4 max concurrently as appropriate; enforce spawn safety distance, pacing and spatial distribution over Z. Balance on readable 2.5D navigation; do not crowd attacks all at once. Target 2–4 minutes **for a reasonable first-time playthrough**, not simulated 40s total campaign. Time target is a design criterion, not an automated sleep gate.
- At least **three differentiated enemy roles** reusing/deriving existing enemy code (e.g. chaser/melee, shield guardian needing guard-break or flank, ranged telegraph unit requiring dodge/depth shift). Meaningful different behavior is required; recolored identical minions do not count.
- Minimum one environmental hazard that communicates its threat and has at least two valid counters (e.g. move out of depth lane, dodge, parry where appropriate). No unavoidable damage.

### D. Combat challenge and boss design (P0)

- Make attack spamming **non-dominant** without simply tripling monster HP: implement telegraphed enemy counterattacks, weapon recovery commitment, guard/parry trade-offs, deliberate dodge iframes, AI attack cooldown/spacing/anti-stunlock. Preserve reasonable responsive controls; never punish input with unfair control lock. Consider enemy poise/armor windows and positional advantage.
- Stage1 boss Commander gets **at least 3 functionally distinct moves**: frontal guard/cleave (positioning), rush/line charge (depth sidestep counter), area shockwave (jump/dash or safe depth lane counter). Explicit startup warning, active collision, recovery punish window; boss behavior must require positioning/defense and Phase 2 (~50% HP) recombination if feasible. No permanent invulnerable boss. Attack frequency and counterplay tested with input timing, not reports alone.
- **Acceptance counterexample:** automated/player trial using only repeated attack button while stationary must fail to clear boss or suffer repeatable punishments; a controlled mixed move/guard/dash/attack trial can win. Do not fake by artificially killing the stationary test hero or triggering direct HP manipulation; use normal AI hits and lifecycle.
- One or two enemies should intentionally sidestep into depth and require player repositioning. All contact and telegraph physics in ground X/Z.
