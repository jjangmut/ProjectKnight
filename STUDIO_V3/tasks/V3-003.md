### Phase A — `ISSUE-V3-001-02` Option C fixed gameplay plane (first, required)

**Code likely involved (inspect source before edit):**

- shared stage base and other subclasses if the shared fix impacts stages 2–5.

**Acceptance behavior:**
1. On Stage 1, player, enemies and boss **spawn and stay on the same gameplay plane `global_position.z=0.0`** (allow a tiny documented physics epsilon); `velocity.z=0` in every mode (idle/move, dash, attack, guard, hurt/knockback, stun/death). Enforce in physics process both before/after movement if collision resolution can induce Z drift; do not mask repeated Z impulses by camera tricks.
2. Keyboard `W/S` or touch joystick vertical movement may no longer shift actor Z or produce depth steering. Keep `move_left`/`move_right` and `jump` producing X/Y actions. **Preserve** airborne attack, dive slam, guard/parry, dash i-frames and combos; resolve any existing `move_down` dive-slam dependency without allowing Z motion.
3. Boss charge, melee overlap/collision layers, directional attack volumes, projectiles/knockback use correct X/Y behavior and do not miss merely because of Z; verify both left/right facing and airborne interactions. Do not just set `global_position.z = 0` and leave stale AI vector Z/pathfinding logic.
4. Keep the 3D geometry, Sprite3D materials/shadows, environment and camera presentation. The Director's Option C allows refining view angle (e.g. -15°..-25°) **only if before/after actual 1280×720 captures show improvement**; do not claim a perfect visual simply from new pitch constant.
5. Stage 1 end-to-end remains traversable; because player/enemy/boss scripts are shared, check at least Stage 2 loading/regression as supported.

**Hard pass:** input-driven Playtest shows stable Z plane during jump, dodge, combat, boss phase and Stage 1 clear; no new collisions or lock-up. A math-only unit test alone is not sufficient.
