## Confirmed source-level leads (reproduce before correcting)

- `CLIENT/Game/scripts/side_quarter_view/bosses/side_qv_crossbow_bolt.gd`: `_physics_process` adds `global_position += direction * (speed*delta)`, then a ray query `PhysicsRayQueryParameters3D.create(prev_pos, global_position, 1 | 4)`; `_on_body_entered` invokes `_impact_and_vanish` for player/world. Inspect whether bolt is hitting invisible `World(4)` collision / paused scene tree / Z alignment; whether visual stays after physics stops; and whether it appears fixed because `look_at`/model orientation is wrong. It should not freeze in mid-air until max range without an explicit gameplay mechanic.
- `CLIENT/Game/scripts/side_quarter_view/stages/side_qv_stage_1.gd`, `_build_pillar`: makes pillar via `CSGBox3D` and adds child to `Node3D` **without explicit `StaticBody3D` or `CollisionShape3D`**, unlike gate barriers. Default CSG collision is often disabled. Inspect all decorative pillars / gothic wing columns and add tight physical collision to clearly solid geometry, with slight player clearance and explicit map lane route. Avoid invisible wide blockers; debug collision overlays and actual moving input tests.
- `side_qv_player.gd` `_clamp_arena_bounds` teleports X/Z bounds after `move_and_slide()`; boss `_physics_process` `move_and_slide()` & movement; hits and dive slam may modify Y. Log before/after physics and jump state; fix only reproducible spurious vertical impulse and avoid simply clamping every Y to zero (breaks intentional jump).
- `CLIENT/Game/tests/v3_021_r1_stage1_natural_e2e.gd` uses `delta=1.0/60` in `SceneTree.process_frame`, and controller reads exact AI states: this test can clear at unrealistically high speed and is not human pacing evidence. Video 29.27s and preceding report 64.13s are not demonstrated to be same run.
