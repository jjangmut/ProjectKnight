## 1. Actual requirement: preserve real 2.5D movement, align fighters during melee

- Side-facing **left/right** sprites must remain during depth traversal; no front/back sprite reintroduction and no giant directional arrow.
- During **close-range offensive interaction** (boss melee telegraph/attack/recovery or hero attacking within valid boss hit range), both characters' **ground contact points should be on approximately the same projected screen-space Y lane**, with visible footing and weapon reach. Initial candidate acceptance: projected foot-Y difference <= 18–25px at 1280x720 in ≥95% of close-range *actively engaging* frames; tune this after examining actual sprite sizes. Record actual foot bbox, **not just CharacterBody3D origin**.
- Achieve this by **boss AI positioning & melee engagement lane matching**, gradually adjusting boss Z toward the hero's Z **when safe**, before close strike, and pausing/facing until aligned. Under pressure / corner conditions, preserve a valid lateral escape route and avoid boss pushing hero to Z boundary. Do not globally lock world Z or teleport hero/boss; avoid scripted hard snap. Boss attack hitbox and active warning should correspond to actual aligned lane (different-Z close-distance should not register invalid unfair hit). Leave depth dodges and Z sidestepping valid defensive options.
- If both characters overlap horizontally at contact, preserve readable hero/boss silhouettes with minimum X spacing appropriate to actual projected sprite bounds and attack reach. Fix targeted occlusion (feet, sword, shield) via real depth, sprite pivot/material handling and contact spacing. Sorting offsets alone are not sufficient. Do not turn collision off, move hero artificially on screen only, or always draw hero in front.
- **Boss retreat/yield must be context-sensitive**: corner escape needs temporary Z separation but *after escape* boss should re-align on player Z before renewed close melee. Audit R2 `player_near_z_wall` logic so boss does not park a whole lane away while still dealing hits.
- Camera should preserve both actors visible but do not remove projection depth entirely or flatten the whole 2.5D scene as an easy cosmetic workaround unless user explicitly requests a camera redesign.
