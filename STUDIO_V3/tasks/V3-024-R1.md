## 4. P0-C: Boss/player leave frame and fight/clear occurs offscreen

Current `side_qv_camera.gd` dual-target mode computes X/Z **midpoint**, adapts FOV based solely on `abs(player.x - boss.x)`, and clamps camera x to `[74,78]` in boss stage; this is **not a guarantee both targets fit onscreen** in a pitched 3D camera, especially when X/Z displacement, edge knockback, camera lag/screen shake, and sprite projected height are involved. Need actual Camera3D projection-based acceptance:
- Each game/render frame use `camera.unproject_position(world_point)` / `camera.is_position_behind` and full projected sprite bounding boxes for BOTH hero and living boss, plus their ground contact and active attack danger zone; track outside-safe-rect frames. Use realistic safe rect e.g. 8–10% margin from screen edges excluding UI; count all out-of-view frames.
- Camera boss combat mode should adapt X center, optional Z focus and FOV/zoom to **keep both fighters visible within safe viewport**, with controlled damping and sensible scene boundary margins. Preserve camera's designed style, no sudden snapping, zoom pumping, or revealing empty world.
- Solve combat bounds **together with** camera bounds; camera-only fixes should not paper over combat characters escaping arena. On boss death, frame hero + kneeling boss + clear banner and hold through victory sequence.
- Include real gameplay test cases: player left edge / boss right edge, reversed; Z opposite lanes, boss shield charge to arena edge, player dash after parry, boss reaches <15% HP, boss dies while near each edge. Screenshot/screen-space telemetry in each case; **natural full run** must have zero fighters outside safe frame while engaged and after clear. A deliberately isolated fixture can force initial positions, clearly labeled as such, but not be passed off as natural E2E.
- Address `stage_min_x/stage_max_x`, boss stage arena bounds, player `_clamp_arena_bounds`, and `SideQVCamera` follow/midpoint without hardcoded decorative position fix.
