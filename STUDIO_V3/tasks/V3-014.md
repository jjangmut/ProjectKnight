## Why this is the next playable feature
The real `side_qv_stage_base.gd::_on_boss_defeated()` currently awards Soul Shards and draws a temporary fixed-position "STAGE CLEARED!" label, then emits `stage_cleared` after a timer. The real `side_qv_campaign.gd::_on_stage_cleared()` automatically enters the next stage 1.8 seconds later. A player cannot pause to understand the reward, see progress through five stages, or choose when to continue. V3-013 already added player-controlled failure/retry and centered responsive overlays; give **successful** stage progression equally clear player agency.
